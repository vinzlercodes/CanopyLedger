# Synthetic reference data card

All checked-in fixtures and demonstration records are synthetic. They are designed to exercise
snapshot immutability, evidence conflicts, CBB/IRC separation, hedge/liquidity, scenarios, climate
evidence state, credit advisory output, human approval, and deterministic reporting.

They are not representative of any borrower, farmer, trading counterparty, warehouse, market feed,
or regulatory conclusion. Do not mix real personal, financial, market, document, geolocation, or
credential data into the reference fixture. Climate/EUDR fields represent evidence readiness only;
they do not establish legal compliance or deforestation-free status.

The credit reference artifact is documented separately in
[`artifacts/credit/reference-v1/model-card.json`](../artifacts/credit/reference-v1/model-card.json).
Its `synthetic_stress_likelihood` remains advisory and cannot modify CBB, IRC, recommendation, or
final-decision authority.
